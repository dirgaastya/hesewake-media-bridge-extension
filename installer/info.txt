Hesewake Media Bridge shows what plays on your Android phone in Hesewake Notch (0.13.0 or newer).

After the install:
  1. In the notch open Settings > Extensions, press Rescan, and turn Media Bridge on.
  2. If Windows asks about the network, allow it for PRIVATE networks only.
  3. Press Settings on the Media Bridge card, then Pair a phone.

This installer needs no administrator rights.
